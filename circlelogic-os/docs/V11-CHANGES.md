# CircleLogic OS v11 - Real Estate Hub ecosystem showcase

## Dashboard data model
The homepage now demonstrates ten data domains that can be synthesised across a real-estate agency's software stack:

1. Financial performance - revenue, GCI, management fees and targets.
2. Sales pipeline and transactions - appraisal through settlement.
3. Enquiries and leads - portal, website, database and open-home demand.
4. Contacts and CRM - buyers, vendors, landlords and past clients.
5. Listings and campaigns - active stock, campaign stage and seller activity.
6. Buyer engagement - illustrative intent, matching and next-best action.
7. Property management - portfolio size, occupancy, arrears, maintenance and inspections.
8. Market and property intelligence - values, comparable-sales context and suburb trends.
9. Marketing performance - views, saves, leads, spend and cost per lead.
10. Diary, tasks and appointments - Google Calendar remains the live source in this build.

## Connector catalogue
The original five connector positions remain first: Agentbox / Reapit Sales, REA Ignite, RP Data / Cotality, Proping and Google Calendar. Their existing setup behaviour is preserved.

The API Connections page also maps 27 additional showcase connectors from the wider Australian real-estate software ecosystem. These are explicitly labelled showcase connectors and do not make provider requests or store provider credentials.

## Executive metrics
The five headline measures are now Revenue MTD, Pipeline GCI, New enquiries, Active listings and Properties managed.
